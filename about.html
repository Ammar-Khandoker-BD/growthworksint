About us

Digital marketing that works as hard as you do

GrowthWorks International is a digital marketing agency built around one promise: Build. Grow. Scale.

Company introduction

One partner for the whole customer journey

We help businesses present themselves well, get found, advertise efficiently, measure what happens and automate the follow-up. Fifteen services cover that journey from the first Facebook Page to server-side tracking.

Our clients range from owners setting up their first online presence to teams running paid campaigns who need accurate data.

Our story

Why GrowthWorks exists

Too many businesses pay separate people for a page, an ad, a website and a tracking tag, and nobody owns the result. GrowthWorks was built to put those pieces under one roof, with one clear scope and one team responsible for how it performs.

Mission

Give every business the digital tools to grow.

We make professional branding, visibility, advertising and tracking accessible and understandable to businesses of any size.

Vision

Businesses that grow, and communities that grow with them.

We want clients to scale confidently and to see growth benefit the people around them.

Core values

What we hold ourselves to

Clarity

Every service has a defined scope and a plain-language outcome. You know what you are buying.

Evidence

We track results and let the data decide what to keep, change or stop.

Integrity

We follow platform rules, and we say so when an outcome is up to the platform and not us.

Care

We treat your business as if it were ours, and we give back through Humanity First.

Our working philosophy

Problem first, tools second

  • Start with the problem. We ask what is not working before we suggest a service.
  • Define done. Each service has a list of deliverables you can check off.
  • Measure it. Pixels, events and reports turn opinions into evidence.
  • Improve continuously. Launch is the start of optimization, not the end.
Our commitment

To you and to Bangladesh

To clients: honest scope, clear communication and work you can verify. To the community: a flat 10% of what we earn goes to people in need through Humanity First.
